HazardGuard Drone

AI-Powered Human Safety System for Drone Delivery

A proposed human-centered safety layer for residential drone delivery. The system monitors the delivery area, detects dynamic hazards, guides the recipient, and pauses delivery when conditions become unsafe.

Project Overview

Safety at the delivery point — not only during flight.

⚠️ The Problem

Residential drone delivery can bring a drone close to people, children, animals, vehicles and other obstacles. The delivery area can become crowded or unpredictable at the moment of collection.

Our approach

HazardGuard Drone adds a safety decision layer that asks:

“Is it safe for people to interact with the drone right now?”

Rather than only caring about the flight path, it monitors the actual handoff environment around the recipient.

Safety Status

The proposed system uses three simple states.

SAFE

The area is clear and the recipient can approach the designated collection zone.

WAIT

Movement or risk is detected. The recipient is kept outside the active collection zone.

STOP

The drone pauses delivery until the environment is safe again.

How It Works

From visual monitoring to safe departure.

01

Monitor

A camera monitors the residential delivery area.

02

Analyze

AI computer vision analyzes the scene in real time.

03

Detect

People, children, animals, vehicles and obstacles are detected.

04

Decide

The safety engine classifies the area as SAFE, WAIT or STOP.

05

Guide

The recipient is guided toward a designated safe position.

06

Collect

The package is collected only when the area is safe.

07

Recheck

The system checks the area again after collection.

08

Depart

The drone departs after the final safety check.

Expected Results

What the proposed system aims to achieve.

Safer Interaction

Reduce unsafe human–drone interactions during residential package collection.

Dynamic Detection

Catch changing hazards such as people entering the delivery zone, moving vehicles, or nearby objects.

Human-Centered Safety

Use a clear, understandable status model so the recipient knows whether it is safe to approach.

Future Development

From concept to working prototype.

Future development will focus on building a working prototype, integrating a camera with an AI-based computer vision model, testing real-time hazard detection, developing the recipient guidance interface, and validating the decision logic in real residential scenarios.
